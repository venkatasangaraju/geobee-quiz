The crust is the outermost solid layer of Earth.
The mantle is the thick layer of hot rock beneath Earth's crust.
The core is the innermost part of Earth made mostly of iron and nickel.
The lithosphere is the rigid outer shell made of the crust and upper mantle.
The asthenosphere is a semi-fluid layer of the upper mantle beneath the lithosphere.
The hydrosphere is all water on Earth, including oceans, rivers, and groundwater.
The atmosphere is the envelope of gases surrounding Earth.
Weather is the short-term condition of the atmosphere at a place and time.
Climate is the long-term average pattern of weather in a region.
Erosion is the process that transports weathered rock and soil.
Weathering is the breakdown of rocks at or near Earth's surface.
A watershed is an area of land where all water drains to a common outlet.
A delta is a landform created by deposition of sediment at a river mouth.
A glacier is a large, slow-moving mass of ice formed from compacted snow.
A plateau is an elevated flat area of land.
An earthquake is the sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement along faults.
A volcano is an opening in Earth's crust through which magma, ash, and gases erupt.
